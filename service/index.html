Our Flight Tours

Our flights operate from Whitehorse Erik Nielsen International Airport using aircraft equipped for wilderness viewing. Every seat has a window. Flights include commentary from experienced bush pilots who know the Yukon landscape intimately. Routes are weather-dependent and adjusted on the day for best visibility.

What's Included

  • Small aircraft with large windows and every-seat-is-a-window
  • Experienced Yukon bush pilots as guides
  • Routes over Kluane National Park, icefields, and the Alaska Highway
  • Glacier landing option for hands-on wilderness experience
  • Flights adjusted daily for weather and visibility
  • Photo-friendly — no wings blocking views in most aircraft
  • Seasonal operation May through September
