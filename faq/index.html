Common Questions

How long are the flights?

Standard flightseeing tours are 1 hour. Extended tours with glacier landing run 2-3 hours. Custom charters are by arrangement.

How much does a flightseeing tour cost?

Standard 1-hour tours start from $295 per person. Glacier landing tours are higher. Contact us for current pricing.

Is flightseeing safe?

Yes. All flights are operated by Transport Canada licensed pilots with extensive Yukon experience. Aircraft are maintained to the highest safety standards.

What happens if the weather is poor?

Flights require reasonable visibility and safe flying conditions. If weather is unsuitable, we reschedule or refund. You only pay for flights that operate.

Can I take photos?

Absolutely. Flightseeing is a photographer's dream. We choose aircraft with minimal wing obstruction and plan routes with photo opportunities in mind.

Is there a weight or age limit?

Children are welcome. There are weight limits for aircraft balance — we will advise when booking. The experience is suitable for most ages.
