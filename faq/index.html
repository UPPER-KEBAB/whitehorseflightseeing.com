Flightseeing planning guide

Good questions. Clearer plans.

In this guide

PlanningAboutQuestionsEnquiriesSeasonsCostsTrip questions
Is a landing included?＋

Only if the operator’s written itinerary says so. Ask whether conditions can prevent it and how that affects the agreed experience and price.

Can I choose my seat?＋

Ask about seat allocation and visibility. Aircraft loading and operational requirements may take priority over preferences.

Will the flight follow the advertised route?＋

Confirm how the operator handles route changes for weather and operating conditions, and which alternatives you would accept.
