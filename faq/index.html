What does Whitehorse flightseeing usually cost?＋

Typical Yukon flightseeing runs CAD 399–1,500 per person, with private aircraft, landings and extended routes pricing higher. Send the inquiry with route, date window and party size for a quote on your dates.

What flightseeing route should I ask for?＋

Start with the view you want: glacier, mountain, Kluane direction, scenic circuit or landing option. WH-MICRO routes the quote request from those choices and keeps the remaining step inside the inquiry.

Can I request premium flightseeing?＋

Yes. Select the premium landing route or multi-flight itinerary variant when you need a private pace, landing option, extended route or photography-first timing. Quotes for those dates come through the inquiry.
