Whitehorse Flightseeing

See the Yukon from the air — glaciers, mountains, and vast wilderness.

The Yukon wilderness is vast, and some of its greatest features are only accessible by air. Our flightseeing tours from Whitehorse take you over Kluane National Park, the world's largest non-polar icefields, and the Alaska Highway corridor. This is a perspective on Yukon you cannot get from the ground.
