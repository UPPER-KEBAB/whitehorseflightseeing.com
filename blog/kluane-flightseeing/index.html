Flightseeing planning guide

Before you go: flight notes

In this guide

PlanningAboutQuestionsEnquiriesSeasonsCostsTrip questions

Start with your priorities

Think beyond the window seat. Plan the route, the aircraft questions and the flexibility a flightseeing trip needs.

The itinerary

Ask what route is proposed, what may change with conditions and whether a landing is actually included.

The aircraft

Confirm boarding arrangements, seating, visibility and any weight or baggage information the operator requires.

The decision to fly

Understand weather postponements, the go/no-go decision, alternate routes and the refund or rescheduling policy.

Take the questions with you

Use the trip checklist to keep the important questions together. Confirm the answers directly with your chosen provider before making a commitment.
